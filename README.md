# Big Data Challenge 2025: Youth Substance Use in Canada

## Overview

Substance use among youth remains a significant public health issue, affecting both individual and community well-being. This project examines patterns of substance use among Canadian teenagers using data from surveys such as the Canadian Student Tobacco, Alcohol, and Drugs Survey (CSTADS). The goal is to identify the factors that influence substance abuse and better understand which populations may be at higher risk.

## Research Focus

This analysis explores how demographic, socioeconomic, health, and behavioral factors contribute to substance use among adolescents in Canada. By examining variables such as:

- age and demographic characteristics
- mental and physical health ratings
- socioeconomic indicators
- substance use behaviors

we aim to uncover the underlying patterns associated with youth substance use.

## Methods

The project uses machine learning models to analyze the survey data, including:

- Random Forest Classifier
- XGBoost

These models help identify the most influential variables and assess how strongly different factors are associated with substance use patterns.

## Key Findings

The analysis suggests a strong relationship between mental and physical health and substance use behavior among youth. It also indicates that socioeconomic, demographic, and behavioral factors play an important role in shaping substance use patterns. These findings highlight the complexity of youth substance use and suggest that risk is influenced by a combination of health status and broader social conditions.

## Limitations

Although the models show promise, their accuracy is limited by the demographic representation of the dataset and the constraints of model training. The available data may not fully capture the diversity of Canadian youth, which can reduce generalizability and predictive performance.

## Future Work

Future research could improve model accuracy by incorporating more comprehensive and diverse datasets. It is also important to ensure privacy and ethical considerations are maintained when handling sensitive health and substance-use data. Expanding the dataset and strengthening responsible data practices could improve both the quality of the analysis and the usefulness of the findings.

## Impact

This project provides valuable insight for healthcare providers, public health professionals, and policymakers seeking to identify high-risk populations and design targeted interventions. By understanding the factors associated with youth substance use, researchers and practitioners can better support prevention and early intervention efforts.

## Repository Purpose

This repository contains the data analysis and modeling work associated with this research topic. The project focuses on using machine learning to investigate youth substance use patterns and the social and health variables that influence them.
